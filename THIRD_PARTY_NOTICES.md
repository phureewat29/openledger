By iApp Technology, Apache License 2.0. The model files in `models/ocr/` are distributed as published at

## OpenCV

https://github.com/opencv/opencv, Copyright the OpenCV contributors, Apache License 2.0 (text in
`models/ocr/LICENSE`). `src/ocr/image/` and `src/ocr/geometry/` reproduce the behaviour of OpenCV's resize, warp,
colour conversion, border, contour, convex hull, minimum-area rectangle and polygon fill routines, so that the port
reads pages exactly as the Python runtime does.
